   bowl, its charges and its share of the spring do not fly. Give growth per 3.276 yr, doubling time

   state the methalox exhaust speed and stage dry fraction used.

   Our rough estimate, not for print: from the 10-day orbit's ~10.6 km/s periapsis, leaving at 11.9 to

   cycle. If so, also give the Earth-launched mass per cycle that holds the fleet level.
